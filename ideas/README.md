# Experiment idea backlog

A broad notebook: ordinary cooking, unusual techniques, historic food processing, instrumented trials, and speculative food materials all belong here. An idea is not a claim that an experiment works.

## Extract, separate, recombine
- Centrifuge-clarified tea, coffee, lemon, orange, tomato; compare before and after concentration or gelation.
- Controlled tea cream / cold haze versus air-interface tea skin formation.
- Compare solids and oil fractions of coconut milk and nut milks.
- Starch sediment from ginger or potatoes; use it in a second preparation.

## Crystal engineering
- Rock candy: sugar supersaturation versus colour or acid additions.
- Honey crystallization: seeded versus unseeded texture and flow.
- Cocoa butter tempering and fat bloom.
- Edible wax oleogels: compare cooling rates and oil binding.
- Humidity-sensitive sugar mixtures and controlled crystal dissolution.

## Enzymes, structure and colour
- Ginger-milk gelation with fresh versus heat-inactivated extract.
- Fresh-pineapple protease effects on suitable protein gels.
- Citrus pectin and enzyme-driven cloud/gel changes.
- Garlic greening and enzyme-triggered precursors (historical food chemistry).
- Oxidative browning with ascorbic-acid delay controls.
- Safe anthocyanin colour gradients from changes in food-grade acidity.

## Ordinary cooking belongs here too
- Bread hydration and proofing comparisons.
- Roasting versus steaming and resulting moisture/texture.
- Coffee extraction temperature, grind and filtration comparisons.
- Chocolate or confection cooling, crystallization and texture.
- Historical recipe reproductions, documenting which parts work in a modern kitchen.

## Potential complex builds
- [EXP-001 Citrus Geode](../experiments/EXP-001-citrus-geode.md): two structural networks and selective enzymatic degradation.
- A moisture-responsive multilayer confection.
- Two-stage crystallization followed by controlled melting or dissolution.
- A structure that changes shape as an edible hydrogel swells.

Move an idea into [experiments](../experiments/README.md) when it has a testable question and a workable control. Keep long-term speculative builds here until the component mechanisms are demonstrated.
