# Awesome Cooking by the Book

Curated references for **experimental cooking**, ordinary recipes, scientific techniques, fermentation, equipment, and publishing. Inspired by [Awesome Recipes](https://github.com/bbbenji/awesome-recipes), but curated for this project's goals rather than copied.

## Recipe collections and publishing

- [Awesome Recipes](https://github.com/bbbenji/awesome-recipes) — an extensive categorized directory; useful for discovering worthwhile food repositories.
- [gitFOOD](https://github.com/foodgit/foodgit.github.io) — Markdown recipes and workflows that produce a recipe website and downloadable books.
- [panozzaj/recipes](https://github.com/panozzaj/recipes) — an example of a personal collection maintained in version control.
- [Chowdown](https://github.com/clarklab/chowdown) — publishing recipes from Markdown.

## Cooking experimentally

- [The Sourdough Framework](https://github.com/hendricius/the-sourdough-framework) — an evidence-informed open book emphasizing underlying baking mechanisms.
- [The Bread Code](https://github.com/hendricius/the-bread-code) — older project with controlled A/B baking experiments; now superseded by the framework.
- [Open Source Bagels](https://github.com/andrewkern/bagels) — open recipe refinement.

## Tools and process control

- [Cooklang specification](https://github.com/cooklang/spec) — structured recipe markup for ingredients and steps.
- [Fermentabot](https://github.com/ariellejohnson/fermentabot-1.0-a-simple-fermentation-box) — a simple controlled-environment fermentation box for koji and related trials.
- [Bar Assistant](https://github.com/karlomikus/bar-assistant) — structured beverage recipe management.

## Food chemistry to explore

- Fat and wax crystallization, tempering and polymorphism
- Fruit and vegetable clarification; centrifugation and sedimentation
- Enzyme-triggered gels, protein restructuring and plant pigments
- Fermentation, acidity, starch gelatinization and retrogradation
- Moisture-driven transformations and sequential reactions

## Curation rules

Keep links useful and current. For each link, explain **why** it is worth following. Prefer original documentation over summaries, and primary research when making a scientific claim. Link to other people's material rather than copying their recipes, text or photos into this repository without permission or a compatible license.
