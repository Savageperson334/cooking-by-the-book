What's actually calculated?

Each time step conserves sensible heat across a 1D half-thickness mesh. Interior heat conduction is coupled to moisture diffusion; surface vapor loss consumes latent heat. Material heat capacities and conductivities vary with composition and temperature. Gelatinization, protein setting and browning are approximate kinetic indicators, not laboratory-validated predictions. Gas production and bubble retention are empirical proxies; a pan-constrained effective-thickness approximation couples gas-driven expansion back to heat flow. There is no validated rheology, free-loaf deformation, internal vapor convection, or microbial safety model. CO₂ escape is not deducted from the mass-loss estimate.
